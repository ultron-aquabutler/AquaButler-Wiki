## What you'll need (preview)

1. Mount enclosure (template + 4× lag bolts)
2. Wire AC (auto-sensing, no voltage set)
3. Connect RS-485 to pool bus via M12 gland
4. Connect WiFi antenna (or wired Ethernet)
5. Power on; rotary display shows `SERVICE MODE` entry prompt
6. Hold >3s for service mode — enter PIN (MMDD)
7. From the customer portal, scan QR on enclosure → registers the unit
